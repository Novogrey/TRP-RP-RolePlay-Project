Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how TRP RP Verification processes information when a user chooses to verify a Roblox account and link it to a Discord account used in the TRP RP roleplay community.

2. Information We Process

The verification process may receive and process:

  • the user's Roblox account ID, username, display name, profile URL, and avatar;
  • the Discord account ID associated with the verification request;
  • the verification result, account-link status, and related timestamps;
  • temporary security data required to complete the OAuth flow.

The application requests only the Roblox OAuth scopes openid and profile. It does not request a Roblox password, payment information, or access to private Roblox messages.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

  • confirm that the user controls the selected Roblox account;
  • link that Roblox account to the user's Discord account;
  • provide verification-based access and project functionality;
  • prevent duplicate links, impersonation, fraud, and abuse;
  • maintain the security and integrity of TRP RP systems.

4. OAuth Tokens

Roblox access and refresh tokens are used only to retrieve the authorized account information. They are not stored in the TRP RP database or in Google Sheets and are revoked after the verification data has been transferred to the bot.

5. Storage and Retention

The Roblox-to-Discord account link is stored in TRP RP's database for as long as it is required to provide verification and community access. Temporary OAuth requests expire automatically after ten minutes. Records may be removed when the link is deleted, when the account is no longer eligible for project access, or when retention is no longer necessary for security and administration.

6. Sharing and Sale of Information

TRP RP does not sell personal information and does not use verification information for advertising. Information is transmitted only through the services required to complete and maintain verification, including Roblox, Discord, Google Apps Script, and the infrastructure used to operate the TRP RP bot and database. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to protect users and the service.

7. User Choices

A user may decline the Roblox authorization request. In that case, OAuth verification is not completed. Requests concerning an existing account link or stored verification record can be submitted to the TRP RP administration through the official Discord server.

8. Security

TRP RP uses limited OAuth scopes, short-lived verification requests, PKCE, restricted application secrets, and access controls intended to protect verification data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable measures are maintained to reduce unauthorized access and misuse.

9. Third-Party Services

Roblox, Discord, Google, and infrastructure providers process information under their own privacy policies. TRP RP does not control the independent privacy practices of those services.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the verification system, legal requirements, or security practices. The effective date shown above identifies the latest published version.

11. Contact

Privacy questions and data requests can be submitted to the TRP RP administration through the official Discord server: discord.gg/gbjxtVF6bA.
