OFFICIAL ACCOUNT VERIFICATION

Verify your Roblox account

Confirm your Discord identity, authorize your Roblox account on the official Roblox page, and link both accounts to TRP RP.

Start verification Join the Discord server

You must be a member of the TRP RP Discord server before starting.

VERIFICATION PROCESS

Three secure steps

  1. 01

    Join TRP RP in Discord

    Membership is required so the bot can apply verification roles and link the correct account.

  2. 02

    Confirm your Discord identity

    Discord provides only your basic account identity. No server-management permissions are requested.

  3. 03

    Authorize through Roblox

    Roblox asks for the openid and profile scopes. Your password is never shared with TRP RP.

DATA AND SECURITY

Only the information required for verification

Received

Discord account ID, Roblox account ID, username, and verification result.

Not received

Passwords, private messages, payment details, or private Roblox data.

OAuth tokens

Used only during authorization, never stored in the project database, and revoked after verification.
